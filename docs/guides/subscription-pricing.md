# Subscription & Pricing

Atomik Trading has three plans: **Builder**, **Pro** and **Elite**. Every plan is full access from day one and includes a paper account, with simulated money and real market prices. Your first payment is covered by a **7-day money-back guarantee**. Prices are flat: no per-trade fees and no share of your results.

**Builder automates your own signals. Pro adds strategies with a live record you can check.**

### AI credits

AI work is billed in credits, priced by what each action actually costs:

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Build a new strategy | 3 |
| Adjust an existing strategy | 1 |
| **Ask a question about your strategy** | **0, always included** |

Credits reset at the start of each billing month. Each plan also has a daily
ceiling so a single busy day can't consume the whole month.

## Which plan is right for you?

**Builder: automate your own signals.** Build strategies with the AI Strategy Builder, backtest them, and run them on paper. Then run up to 3 of your own strategies live on Atomik's engine, or send your own signals from TradingView alerts (or anywhere else) to up to 3 webhook strategies, across up to 3 live broker accounts. Copy trading is included inside the plan: one leader account copying to two followers. Manual trading from the chart works on every account.

Builder doesn't include marketplace strategies (subscribing to or buying anyone else's strategy), selling on the marketplace, ARIA Research, or Interactive Brokers.

**Pro: add strategies with a live record you can check.** Everything in Builder, plus marketplace strategies: each one has a record of every trade from its own live signals, and Atomik's engine runs it in your account. Pro also adds up to 10 live accounts, 10 webhook strategies, 15 strategies running at once, copy trading with 3 groups and 10 followers, ARIA Research, Interactive Brokers, selling on the marketplace, and the premium AI model for longer, more intricate strategies.

**Elite: done for you.** For when you'd rather not do the setup or the watching yourself. We set it up with you on a call, follow up on order problems on your account, send a weekly report on your strategies, and you get a direct line to the founder. Plus 25 accounts, unlimited webhook strategies and strategies, and unlimited copy trading groups.

## 7-day money-back guarantee

There is no free plan and no trial. The money-back guarantee is how you try Atomik:

- Full access to all features of your plan from the moment you subscribe
- If Atomik isn't for you, refund your first payment within 7 days of it from **Settings → Billing**, or email [support@atomiktrading.io](mailto:support@atomiktrading.io)
- Cancel anytime: you keep access through the end of the billing period you've paid for

## Managing your subscription

### Upgrade or downgrade

You can change your plan at any time:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing** in your dashboard
2. Click **Manage Subscription**
3. Select your new plan

Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

### Cancel

1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription**
2. Click **Cancel Plan**
3. You retain access until the end of your current billing period

### Billing portal

Billing runs through Whop, our payment processor. Click **Manage Subscription** to open your Whop billing page, where you can:

- Update payment method
- View invoice history
- Download receipts
- Change billing cycle

## Payment failure handling

If a payment fails (expired card, insufficient funds, etc.):

1. **Grace period**: you retain full access while we retry the payment
2. **Warning**: email notification about the failed payment
3. **Urgent**: follow-up notification if still unresolved
4. **Final notice**: last chance to update your payment method
5. **Suspended**: account access is restricted until payment is resolved

**To resolve:** Update your payment method in **Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription**. Once payment succeeds, full access is immediately restored.

## FAQ

**Is there a free plan or a free trial?**
No. Every plan is paid, and every plan includes a paper account. Your first payment has a 7-day money-back guarantee: if Atomik isn't for you, refund it within 7 days.

**What's the difference between Builder and Pro?**
Builder automates your own signals: strategies you build in the Strategy Builder and signals you send from TradingView or anywhere else. Pro adds marketplace strategies with a live record you can check, plus more accounts, ARIA Research and Interactive Brokers.

**Can I switch plans mid-cycle?**
Yes. Upgrades are prorated and take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the next billing date.

**What happens to my strategies if I downgrade?**
If your new plan has lower limits, excess strategies and webhooks will be deactivated. You choose which ones to keep. Moving from Pro to Builder also stops any marketplace strategies you run.

**Is there a refund policy?**
Yes. Your first payment has a 7-day money-back guarantee: refund it from **Settings → Billing** within 7 days, or contact [support@atomiktrading.io](mailto:support@atomiktrading.io). After that, refunds are evaluated case by case.

**Do yearly plans auto-renew?**
Yes. You can cancel anytime before renewal through the billing portal.
